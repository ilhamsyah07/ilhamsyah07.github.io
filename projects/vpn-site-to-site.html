Latar BelakangBackground

Perusahaan memiliki beberapa kantor cabang di lokasi berbeda. Sebelumnya komunikasi antar cabang melewati internet publik tanpa enkripsi, sehingga berisiko terhadap intercept data. Solusi VPN lama berbasis IPSec dinilai rumit dikonfigurasi dan overhead-nya tinggi. The company had multiple branch offices in different locations. Previously, inter-branch communication traveled over the public internet without encryption, exposing data to interception risks. The legacy IPSec-based VPN solution was complex to configure and had high overhead.

Proyek ini memigrasikan infrastruktur VPN ke WireGuard — protokol VPN modern yang lebih ringan, lebih cepat, dan jauh lebih mudah dikonfigurasi. Tunnel dibangun antara kantor pusat dan setiap cabang menggunakan public-key cryptography sehingga seluruh traffic internal berjalan terenkripsi. This project migrated the VPN infrastructure to WireGuard — a modern VPN protocol that is lighter, faster, and far easier to configure. Tunnels were built between the headquarters and each branch using public-key cryptography, ensuring all internal traffic runs encrypted.
